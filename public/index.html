A SMALL DISCOVERY

I didn’t study design.
I started noticing it.

Why does one package make you pick it up? Why does a colour feel loud, calm, expensive, playful? Why does one homepage make sense in seconds while another makes you work for it?

I started paying attention to the choices behind things I already liked. No formal design degree handed me that lens. I built it by looking, making, getting feedback, and trying again.
